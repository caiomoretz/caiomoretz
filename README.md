## About me

I am an economist and PhD candidate at FGV EPGE in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. My research examines how environmental policies and land institutions shape deforestation, land use, and economic behavior, with a particular focus on the Brazilian Amazon. I combine causal-inference methods with geospatial and administrative data.

## Research interests

- Environmental economics
- Applied microeconomics
- Development economics
- Deforestation and land-use change
- Environmental regulation and land institutions
- Policy evaluation

## Current research

- **Protected Area Erasure Accelerates Deforestation in the Brazilian Amazon**, with Francisco Costa. Revise and resubmit at the *Journal of the Association of Environmental and Resource Economists*. [Working paper](https://osf.io/preprints/socarxiv/wgkty_v1)
- **Between Regularization and Contestation: Brazil's Rural Environmental Registry and Land-Use Change in the Amazon**. Work in progress.

## Methods and tools

My empirical work combines causal inference, panel-data methods, and geospatial analysis applied to large administrative datasets. I work primarily in R, Stata, and Google Earth Engine, with additional experience in QGIS, Julia, and Python.
