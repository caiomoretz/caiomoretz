# Caio Moretz-Sohn

### Economist · PhD Candidate in Economics at [FGV EPGE](https://epge.fgv.br/en)

I research environmental and land-use policy using tools from Applied Microeconomics and causal inference, combining administrative, geospatial, and remote-sensing data to study deforestation, conservation, land governance, and related policy questions, particularly in the Brazilian Amazon. Beyond academia, I contribute to applied policy projects on forest conservation and restoration, territorial planning, and environmental governance in Brazil.
