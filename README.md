## About me

I am an economist and PhD candidate at FGV EPGE in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. My research examines how environmental policies and land institutions shape deforestation, land use, and economic behavior, with a particular focus on the Brazilian Amazon. I combine causal-inference methods with geospatial and administrative data. I also work as an independent consultant in environmental economics, climate finance, and forest policy.

## Research interests

- Applied Microeconomics
- Environmental Economics
    - Deforestation and land-use change
    - Environmental regulation and institutions
- Development Economics

## Current research

- **Protected Area Erasure Accelerates Deforestation in the Brazilian Amazon**, with Francisco Costa. First dissertation chapter; revise and resubmit at the *Journal of the Association of Environmental and Resource Economists*. The paper estimates the effects of protected area downsizing and degazettement on deforestation and conversion to pasture, cropland, and mining. [Working paper](https://osf.io/preprints/socarxiv/wgkty_v1)
- **Between Regularization and Contestation: Brazil's Rural Environmental Registry and Land-Use Change in the Amazon**, with Francisco Costa. Second dissertation chapter in progress. The project studies how registration in Brazil's Rural Environmental Registry affects deforestation and land-use change, including heterogeneity under competing land claims.
