# Caio Moretz-Sohn

### Economist · PhD Candidate in Economics at [FGV EPGE](https://epge.fgv.br/en)

I am an economist and PhD candidate in Economics at FGV EPGE, with research focused on Environmental Economics. My work examines how environmental regulation, land institutions, and climate-related policies shape land use and economic outcomes in Brazil, applying causal inference methods to large-scale administrative and geospatial datasets. I also work as an independent consultant on environmental policy, forest conservation and restoration, and climate finance, contributing to applied research and policy projects across the public sector, private sector, and civil society.
